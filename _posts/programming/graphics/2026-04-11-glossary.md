## 2020 edition

light transport
: buses, trams and low capacity rail

Metropolis light transport
: art deco buses, trams and low capacity rail. gecko dancing robot optional but recommended

BRDF
: Bordigist Red Dissenter Faction, a minor left communist group with six members, who formally disbanded in 1995

baking
: very important for scenes containing bread

retopology
: the academic study of experimenting with a receptive role in gay sex, but finding it disappointing

shader
: a small program designed to run in parallel, which takes in vertex data and uses it to say cruel things to your enemies

triangle
: a musical instrument

keyframe
: a small gilded rectangle for displaying encryption data

deferred rendering
: when you forget to render your project before the deadline

rigging
: used to swing aboard an adjacent animation studio

NURBS
: not an acronym, just stated with the proper emphasis

raymarching
: a variant of raytracing usually seen at parades and sporting events

chromatic aberration
: the offspring of a red dragon and a mind flayer

PBR
: parboiled rice, used primarily as a religious offering in the context of computer graphics

Bezier curve
: the number of years it would take you to earn the current net worth of Amazon CEO Jeff Bezos, thereby becoming more Bezy. monotonically increasing with time.

### supplemental: a guide to shaders

vertex shader
: a waterproof, breathable type of shader used to render outdoor clothing

fragment shader
: used by witches to establish complex metafictional games

pixel shader
: pseudonym used by fragment shaders rendering genre fiction

geometry shader
: after widespread condemnation of the geometry shader’s untoward remarks about the GPU pipeline put an end to its career in graphics, it has sadly fallen into the sordid world of right-wing podcasts and tabloid columns

hull shader
: a niche type of shader, primarily used by residents of yorkshire

domain shader
: rejected proposal to speed up internet address resolution with GPUs

compute shader
: the swiss army knife of shaders. literally; it has a corkscrew and stuff

mesh shader
: mostly used for rendering nightclubs

tensor shader
: shader which contains covariant and contravariant components in a curved space

ray generation shader
: sorts rays into categories like 'millennial’, 'zoomer’ and 'gen alpha’ for appropriately targeted mockery

closest hit shader
: uses the full power of massively parallel computing to look up the nearest weed stash

miss shader
: controversial beauty pageant; currently judged on a combination of categories like variable naming, instruction count and branch coherence
